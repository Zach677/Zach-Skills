# AI Usage Policy

{{PROJECT_NAME}} allows AI assistance, but only with a responsible human in
the loop.

## Rules

- **Disclose AI usage.** State which tools you used and how much of the work
  was AI-assisted.
- **Understand the work.** You must be able to explain your change, the
  relevant tradeoffs, and the effects on the broader project without relying
  on an AI tool during review.
- **Review and edit AI-assisted writing.** Issues, discussions, and pull
  requests may use AI assistance, but the final text must be reviewed,
  corrected, and tightened by a human.
- **Do not submit AI-generated media.** Code and text are the only AI-assisted
  content allowed under this policy unless a maintainer explicitly approves an
  exception.
- **Low-quality AI use may lead to denouncement.** Contributors who submit
  obvious AI slop, cannot explain their work, or repeatedly ignore this policy
  may be added to the public denouncement list.

These rules apply to outside contributions to {{PROJECT_NAME}}. Maintainers
may use AI tools at their discretion because they are accountable for project
quality.

## There Are Humans Here

Every issue, discussion, and pull request creates review work for people.
Low-effort AI output shifts the burden of understanding and validation onto
maintainers, which is not acceptable.

AI tools can produce useful work when guided by someone who understands the
project. They can also produce confident, noisy, or incorrect output. The
human contributor is responsible for telling the difference before asking for
review.

## AI Is Welcome Here

This policy is not anti-AI. AI can be a productive tool for research, coding,
debugging, and editing. The problem is not the tool; the problem is submitting
work that the contributor does not understand.

Use AI if it helps you, disclose it clearly, and be ready to own the result.
