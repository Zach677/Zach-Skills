# Topic Selection

Use this when choosing the daily topic. Scripts gather candidates and trend
signals; the agent still makes the editorial decision.

## Current Traffic Assumptions

- WeChat Official Account distribution is now a mixed private-subscription and
  public-recommendation system. Title, cover, completion, interaction, search
  keyword match, and topic tags all matter.
- Hot topics are not noise by default. Social, entertainment, and vertical
  hotspots can all work if the article gives the reader a clear reason to open,
  forward, or search.
- The target reader is still Zach's account audience, but the opening hook can
  ride a broader hot topic before landing in practical value.

## Traffic Lanes

Prefer one of these lanes when the trend scan shows momentum:

- Public order and official response: official notices, rumor clarification,
  service changes, public safety, new rules.
- Health life and seasonal care: weather, hospitals, chronic disease routines,
  sleep, diet, heat, rain, medicine, family care.
- Consumer safety and practical avoidance: food, refunds, phone/WeChat/payment
  safety, travel purchases, repair, insurance, fake notices.
- Family emotion and relationships: parents, children, retirement, caregiving,
  household conflict, intergenerational boundaries.
- Tech livelihood: AI, phones, WeChat, apps, vehicles, robots, and consumer tech
  only when the angle is everyday impact.
- Travel weather and public services: traffic, rail, flights, scenic spots,
  holidays, weather disruptions.
- Culture hotspot: sports, film, music, short drama, and public figures only
  when the article can extract a useful family or public-life angle.

## Trend Chase Rules

- Scan periodically, usually every 2-4 hours during waking hours, and compare
  the latest snapshot against the previous one.
- Prefer rising topics over topics that are already saturated, unless the topic
  has a new official response, new data, or a fresh practical angle.
- A topic becomes publishable when it has at least two of these: clear traffic
  signal, rising rank/heat, search-friendly keywords, family-forwarding value,
  or credible current sources.
- Do not force all topics into health. A hot public-service, consumer, family,
  or tech-livelihood angle may be better than a weak health topic.

## Down-rank

- Pure AI product news, model updates, startup gossip, or developer tools unless
  the everyday consumer impact is obvious.
- Entertainment gossip with no transferable lesson, no public-interest hook,
  and no safe practical angle.
- Highly negative social incidents where the only appeal is outrage, fear, or
  speculation.
- Medical, legal, financial, or policy claims that cannot be sourced
  conservatively.

## Minimum Checks

- What is the traffic reason to open this today?
- Is the topic rising, newly official, or still search-active?
- What can the reader retell or forward in one sentence?
- Is there a credible source for every risky claim?
- Is this too close to recent article history?
