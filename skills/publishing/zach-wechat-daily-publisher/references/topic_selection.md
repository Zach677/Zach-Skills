# Topic Selection

Use this when choosing the daily topic. The script can export history, but the
agent should make the final editorial choice.

## Default Lane

- Middle-aged and older readers, roughly 45+.
- Family caregivers who forward practical reminders to parents.
- Low comprehension cost and clear everyday value.

## Strong Topic Shapes

- Health and seasonal care: sleep, diet, walking, joints, blood sugar,
  digestion, heat, rain, cold waves, medicine storage.
- Silver-life services: retirement, community services, family doctor,
  certification, appointment, travel, official channels.
- Anti-scam and personal-info safety: phone calls, QR codes, face scan,
  ID copies, payment codes, fake official notices.
- Practical consumer reminders: food safety, appliance safety, travel, tickets,
  refund, repair, public-service entry points.
- Human-interest or family-life topics only when they land in a practical
  action or boundary reminder.

## Down-rank

- Pure AI product news, model updates, startup gossip, developer tools.
- Entertainment gossip, young internet slang, fandom topics.
- Abstract social commentary with no family action.
- Finance/legal/medical/policy claims that require professional advice unless
  the article can stay at official-source, low-risk, general-information level.

## Minimum Checks

- What can the reader do today?
- Can the point be retold in one sentence inside a family group chat?
- Is there a credible source for every risky claim?
- Is this too close to recent article history?
- Is the title understandable without prior technical context?

## If Hotspot Discovery Is Weak

Do not force a noisy hot-search item. Pick a timely evergreen topic from:

- current season and weather
- recent official guidance
- public-health calendar days
- family safety and anti-scam reminders
- common consumer or service-verification pain points
